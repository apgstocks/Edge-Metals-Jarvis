Two separate defects in one line, and the item was FACTUALLY CORRECT
throughout -- which is the point. Being right is not the same as being
worth an interruption.

1. THE "!!" AND THE "by 9/17 (today)" CAME FROM AN ESTIMATED READY DATE.

   That is when the cargo is ready. It is a property of the shipment, not
   a date by which anybody must act, and nothing expires when it passes.
   So the loudest line in her digest was urgent about nothing.

   The prompt invited it -- "deadline: any date or time limit the sender
   actually states" -- so the model answered the question it was asked.
   The prompt is tightened, but the structural rule is in code, for the
   same reason as HEADERS BEAT THE MODEL: the judgement is the model's,
   the check is something the code can verify.

   deadlineIsShipmentDate() drops a deadline when the sentence it appears
   in describes the shipment (ERD, earliest return date, estimated ready
   date, ETD, ETA, arrival, departure, sailing) and says nothing must
   happen by it.

   A CUTOFF SURVIVES. Miss an SI cutoff and the container does not sail;
   it is the hardest deadline in this business, and it is protected by
   OBLIGATION_WORD -- not, as my comment first implied, by being absent
   from the descriptive list. Reverse-verification found that: adding
   "cutoff" to SHIPMENT_DATE breaks no test, because the obligation check
   rescues the sentence anyway. The comment now says which guard holds.

   TWO OF MY OWN MISTAKES, both found by testing against the real text:

     * The first version compared the deadline's TOKENS against each
       sentence. The model NORMALISES the date -- it returned "9/17"
       while the body said "September 17" -- so nothing matched and the
       guard silently did nothing on the exact case it was written for.
       Both sides are parsed and the DAY compared now (AJ2).
     * Sentence scoping was load-bearing but untested: AJ8 passed even
       with whole-message matching, because its deadline sentence happens
       to contain "by". AJ9 isolates it with a real ask that has no
       obligation word and a sailing in another sentence.

2. AN OWED ITEM OUR OWN TEAM RAISED IS THEIR WORK, NOT HERS.

   Accounting Edge asked Zimex for a booking and Zimex has not answered,
   so Zimex genuinely owes us something. isOwedItem was right. But Bose
   raised it, Bose is chasing it, and she has no action.

     Apsara: "they just asked for booking. why it needs to come to
              whatsapp? no.."

   fromInternal was already computed in assess() and used only for
   is_order; it is now carried out as `from_internal`, and it separates
   the two shapes of waiting_on:'them' exactly:

     OUR outbound request   "we asked Zimex for a booking"   -> dropped
     THEIR inbound report   "Andy is chasing the carrier for the EDO"
                            -> kept. A live commitment made TO us is the
                            most useful thing in the inbox, and it is the
                            case this bucket was built for.

   Undefined from_internal (older stored items, fixtures) reads as
   external and is KEPT -- failing towards showing her something she can
   dismiss, never towards silence. And the suppression is logged by name
   every time: the PO and importance gaps went unnoticed for weeks
   precisely because nothing was written down when mail was dropped.
