FOUR SEPARATE DEFECTS, and only the last is cosmetic.

1. A FALSE `change` ALERT, FIRED TWICE IN ONE NIGHT.

   The same line on two different emails two hours apart:

     (!) change+cutoff+instruction: cutoff on DALA61376400: we hold
         09/18/2026, this mail says 09-16

   I opened the mailbox. NEITHER email contains a date at all. The
   "09-16" is buildThreadLedger's OWN row prefix:

     - [09-16] Andy Park: DG SI CUTOFF for this booking is 10AM ...

   So changedBookingDate was reading Jarvis's own bookkeeping as a date
   the sender had stated. Because the rows it scans are the ones naming
   a cutoff, and a cutoff reminder is exactly the kind of row a ledger
   keeps, this would fire on a great many threads -- and `change` is the
   ONLY signal allowed to interrupt her. Worst possible place for a
   false positive.

   Fixed by stripping the "[MM-DD] " prefix, not by dropping the thread:
   a cutoff is often stated once, earlier in the conversation, and
   reading the history is why the ledger is passed in at all (CE3, CE4).
