Every load, with its weights.

Add a purchase or a sale, enter gross and tare, and the net works itself out. Each load keeps its items, its amount and its PDF ticket.

  • Purchases and sales in one list, filtered by month, seller or item
  • Weight photos and the seller's signature stay on the ticket
  • Edit or delete a load and the stock follows it

Payments that settle against the load.

Receive a payment against a sale or pay a supplier against a purchase. The screen shows the total, what has been received and what is still outstanding before you record anything.

  • Cash, bank transfer and the other ways you actually pay
  • An optional reference for the cheque or transaction
  • It tells you when a payment settles the load in full

Stock that matches your loads.

Inventory is worked out live from every current load, so deleting or correcting a load changes the stock with it. Nothing to keep in step by hand.

  • By item type, by day or by seller
  • Gross, net in, shipped and on hand for each item
  • Mark items ferrous or non-ferrous the first time you enter them

And a few things that save time.

The assistant

Type or speak a question about stock, a supplier's balance or a load. Ask it to record a payment and it waits for your confirmation first.

Clean names

Merge spellings of the same supplier, buyer or item so reports stop splitting one name into three.

Phone and web

Use it from a computer in the office or a phone at the scale. Both show the same data.

Before you start

What free means

No card and no billing during the trial. You get your own logins and your own data, and sample data to look through before you enter anything real.

The full terms are in the free-trial agreement you accept at sign-up.

What it does not do

Record-keeping and reporting rules differ by state and city. Y.A.R.D. does not make you compliant on its own, so check what applies to your yard.

A trial ends, and we are inviting a small number of yards first, so there may be a short wait.

Questions

Can another yard see my data?

No. Each trial is a separate copy with its own passwords. Our team can reach the server it runs on.

What happens when the trial ends?

It switches off after , and your data is archived instead of deleted. The agreement you accept at sign-up says what happens to it next.

Do I need a card?

No.

Is there a phone app?

Yes. Use the website on a computer and the app on a phone. Your address arrives when your trial is ready.

Is it only for metal?

It is built for scrap and metal yards: loads, weights, items and payments. If your yard works differently, say so when you ask for a code.

Does it connect to accounting software?

Not in the trial. Tell us what you use when you ask for a code.
