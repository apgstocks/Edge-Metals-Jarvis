## Every change, and the complaint it comes from

| change | why |
|---|---|
| **Sections with headers** instead of `"2 emails waiting on you, you're waiting on 2, and 1 your team is handling"` | You had to parse a sentence of arithmetic before reaching anything real — and tell which bucket each item was in by reading the *tail* of its line |
| **The action is the headline**, the sender moves underneath | Who sent it almost never changes what you do about it |
| **Sender leads for items needing nothing** — "Eccomelt sent…" | "sent Purchase Ticket #4404952" as a headline reads like an instruction and isn't one |
| **One context line per item**, not three or four | Ten items cost 40 lines in the old shape |
| **Names, not addresses** — "Brian (RadMetals)" not `brian@radmetals.com` | You know who Brian is |
| **The `·` mark is gone** | It appeared on almost everything, so it meant nothing. `❗` still means something |
| **Ordered by what it costs you to miss** | You can stop reading at any point and have lost nothing important |
| **One message a day-ish** | 3 items or 3 hours — already built, shipping with this |

---

## What I have deliberately **not** changed

- **Numbering stays contiguous, 1…10, across sections** — so `reply to 7` and `ignore 7` keep working. This is the trap: the list must be *sorted once* and both rendered **and** stored from that same array. Rendering from one order and numbering from another is the bug that once drafted a reply to the wrong customer.
- **Nothing is dropped.** Every item you received is still here, in a different place.
- **The alert window stays on LA time.** Your call, twice.
