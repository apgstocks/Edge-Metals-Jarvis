## Long description

Edge Metals Inc is a Los Angeles-based exporter of ferrous and non-ferrous scrap metal, in business since 2008. We buy from businesses (suppliers, recyclers, dealers and workshops) and export container loads from our yards in Los Angeles and Houston to smelters, refineries and foundries in Korea, Taiwan, India and Pakistan.

Every load is received, sorted and graded at our own yards, weighed and photographed, then loaded into 20' or 40' containers with scale tickets and seal numbers documented. Buyers, brokers and inspectors are welcome by appointment.

Materials: auto cast, shredded auto cast, aluminum wheels (clean and dirty), aluminum engine combo, aluminum breakage, alternators / starters / AC compressors, electric motors, sealed units (compressors), auto harness wire, HMS (heavy melting steel), galvanized steel, cast iron engine blocks, rotors and drums, batteries. Junk cars are bought at our Houston yard only.

We do not buy from walk-ins or individuals.
