THE ROOT CAUSE IS GENERAL, and neither a PO problem nor an email
problem. Jarvis has never measured importance; it measures "does
somebody want a reply from you". Five of the six gate conditions need a
QUOTABLE REQUEST SENTENCE, and freight mail mostly does not ask. It
states a fact with a consequence: a cutoff, a figure, a schedule
change, an instruction.

helpers/mailImportance.js scores that, IN CODE, from the sender's own
sentences, and never from a.summary -- scoring the model's output with
the model's output is the circularity that made `confidence` worthless
(1.0 on 16 of 16 live emails). Every signal must produce a sentence the
sender literally wrote; one that cannot is dropped. Same discipline as
asked_for_quote, same reason (Chain-of-Verification, arXiv:2309.11495:
do not re-ask "are you sure", ask for something checkable).

  critical  a date we HELD has changed, something has broken, or a
            cutoff is inside two days.   -> interrupts her
  high      money, an instruction aimed at us, a cutoff further out, a
            service about to be cut off. -> next digest
  normal    a live reference, nothing sharp. Never promoted.
  low       no grounded signal. Watched, recorded, quiet.

"Notify me straight away" was her explicit choice for consequential
mail that is not hers to answer, so `critical` alone breaks the hourly
rhythm -- never on good news, never outside the alert window.

"IGNORE ALL THE MAILS NOT RELATED TO EDGEMETALS", DONE SAFELY. I nearly

The content cannot settle it either, because these are the same text:

  "Payment ID 4004930 has been issued for $13,992.00"   <- her customer
  "Your invoice INV-4023 for $49.00 is due"             <- her software

What separates them is that she has emailed one and never emailed the
other. sentIndex, senderStats and email_contacts.json already say so,
at no API cost. Admin mail is DEMOTED, never deleted.

TWO OTHER BUGS FIXED ON THE WAY, both found by running this against
real data rather than fixtures:

  * parseDeadline's last resort was a bare `new Date(s)`, and V8 reads
    "september 17" as the YEAR 2001. "September 17" came back -9131
    days: her digest would have printed "OVERDUE by 9131d" and the
    nudge would have fired 25 years late. Worse, "September 17 morning
    at 10 AM" returned NOTHING, so the one cutoff landing that morning
    could not be recognised as imminent. Month names now parse in both
    word orders, with ordinals, with trailing prose, and only ISO
    reaches the built-in parser.

  * `tracked` was keyed on the MESSAGE id, so a second message on a
    thread she had not answered created a SECOND open item. groupMatters
    hides that inside one digest, but collectChaseUps and
    collectDeadlineReminders iterate `tracked` directly with no thread
    dedupe -- three messages from Tiffany on one appointment thread
    became three things chasing her, each with its own age. That is the
    RadMetals 709275/709276 duplicate she sent in. One thread is now one
    open item; the newer message wins the slot; firstFlaggedAt and the
    chase count carry over, because the age of a matter is how long SHE
    has had it and resetting the count would mean MAX_CHASES is never
    reached.

THE REPORT EARNED ITS PLACE TWICE. scripts/importance-report.js scores a
window of real mail and prints what WOULD have happened, and I ran it
before letting any of this notify her:

  1. First run: 37 of 153 critical -- five interruptions a day. Top hit
     was a subject line reading "with erd 9/9", a week in the past,
     which made every cutoff look overdue and therefore imminent. The
     cutoff date now comes from the cutoff SENTENCE, the subject is
     excluded, and a negative value no longer qualifies. 37 -> 17.
  2. Second run listed "Payment for Sealed units $108596.40 paid" from
     Edge Metals Bose under "would have gone quiet". knownCounterpartyTest
     built its domain set from three ledgers that are EMPTY on a fresh
     deployment, so nothing was known -- and the comment on the function
     said "Her own company always counts. Internal mail is never admin."
     while the code did no such thing. Her own domains are passed in now,
     and the report refuses to print the quiet list at all when the
     ledger is thin, rather than inviting a decision off a wrong number.
